Privacy Policy

AR Trafaret works locally on your device. We do not require an account, and we do not upload your camera feed or selected images to our servers.

Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect names, email addresses, account information, location data, contacts, camera images, photos selected by the user, or user-created AR placement content.

Camera Access

AR Trafaret asks for camera access so it can show augmented reality content in the real world. The camera feed is used on your device for AR positioning and preview. Camera images are not uploaded to us, stored by us, or shared with us.

Photo Library Access

AR Trafaret asks for photo library access so you can choose a sketch or image to use as an AR stencil. The selected image is used locally on your device. It is not uploaded to us, stored on our servers, or shared with us.

Local Processing And Local Storage

Image display, opacity changes, scaling, rotation, mirroring, calibration grid display, and AR placement happen locally on your device.

The app may store local settings on your device, such as saved AR placement, opacity, scale, and control preferences. You can remove local app data by deleting the app from your device.

Free Access

AR Trafaret is free to use. The current version does not contain paid features, in-app purchases, subscriptions, import tokens, or advertising.

Third-Party Services

The current version does not use advertising networks, payment services, third-party cloud storage, or account systems.

Tracking

AR Trafaret does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies and does not use advertising identifiers.

Children's Privacy

AR Trafaret does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share personal data.

Data Retention

AR Trafaret does not retain personal data on our servers. Local app data remains on your device until you delete it or delete the app.

Changes To This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the app changes. If we add features or SDKs that collect data, this policy and the App Store privacy information will be updated.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact artrafaret.app@gmail.com.
