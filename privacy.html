Privacy Policy

AR Trafaret is designed to work mostly locally on your device. We do not require an account, and we do not upload your camera feed or selected images to our servers.

Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect names, email addresses, account information, location data, contacts, camera images, photos selected by the user, or user-created AR placement content.

Camera Access

AR Trafaret asks for camera access so it can show augmented reality content in the real world. The camera feed is used on your device for AR positioning and preview. Camera images are not uploaded to us, stored by us, or shared with us.

Photo Library Access

AR Trafaret asks for photo library access so you can choose a sketch or image to use as an AR stencil. The selected image is used locally on your device. It is not uploaded to us, stored on our servers, or shared with us.

Local Processing And Local Storage

Image display, opacity changes, scaling, rotation, mirroring, calibration grid display, and AR placement happen locally on your device.

The app may store local settings on your device, such as import token balance, subscription entitlement state, saved AR placement, opacity, scale, and control preferences. You can remove local app data by deleting the app from your device.

In-App Purchases And Subscriptions

AR Trafaret may offer optional in-app purchases, including import token packs and a monthly unlimited import subscription.

Payments are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment card details. The app may receive purchase confirmation, product identifiers, and subscription entitlement information from Apple so it can unlock the purchased features and restore active subscriptions.

You can manage or cancel subscriptions through your Apple ID subscription settings.

Rewarded Ads

AR Trafaret may show optional rewarded ads that let you earn additional image import tokens.

Rewarded ads are provided by Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK. Google may process information such as device identifiers, ad interaction data, approximate device information, diagnostics, and other information needed to load, measure, limit, and secure ads. Ad availability can vary by region and device.

Rewarded ads are optional. A token is granted only after the rewarded ad completion callback is received by the app.

Third-Party Services

AR Trafaret uses Apple App Store services for in-app purchases and may use Unity In-App Purchasing to connect the app to Apple purchase processing.

AR Trafaret uses Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK for optional rewarded ads.

The current version does not use third-party cloud storage or account systems.

Tracking

AR Trafaret does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies. If an advertising setup requires App Tracking Transparency permission, the app will request permission where required.

Children's Privacy

AR Trafaret does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal data. Purchase processing is handled by Apple through the App Store. Rewarded ads are handled by Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK.

Data Retention

AR Trafaret does not retain personal data on our servers. Local app data remains on your device until you delete it or delete the app. Apple may retain purchase records according to Apple's own policies.

Changes To This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the app changes. If we add features or SDKs that collect data, this policy and the App Store privacy information will be updated.

Contact

For privacy questions or purchase support, contact artrafaret.app@gmail.com.
